Privacy Policy for Arrow Maze

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Maze ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Arrow Maze does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Your game progress (which levels you've completed) is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App displays optional rewarded video ads (for example, to reveal a hint or continue after running out of lives) using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

To serve ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general location, and app usage data, and may use this information to personalize ads or measure ad performance, subject to your device's ad settings and applicable law.

We do not control and are not responsible for how Google or its advertising partners collect or use this data. You can learn more about Google's practices and your choices (including opting out of personalized ads) at:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Arrow Maze is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is no server-side data for us to retain. Locally stored game progress remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

Your Choices

  • You can reset your local game progress by uninstalling and reinstalling the App, or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, "Limit Ad Tracking"/App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
